# Making play accessible without impoverishing it

A game can be difficult to practice because of rules, preparation, improvisation, prior knowledge, coordination, or confidence.

Removing things may reduce that burden. It may also remove what made the experience worth pursuing.

The recurring question is therefore:

> **How can an experience become more accessible without reducing the complexity we wanted to make playable?**

This question led to the distinction between [simplicity, complication and complexity](../simplicity-complexity.md), and to the study of [where load moves](../accessibility.md).

> **Do not simplify the world. Simplify the way into it.**
