# What still makes a role-playing game?

GM. Dice. Character sheet. Spoken conversation. Individual character. Prepared scenario. Campaign.

All belong deeply to RPG history. That does not make each of them necessary.

One way to understand a component is to remove, move, or replace it and observe what function becomes visible.

The goal is less to police or expand a boundary than to learn what established forms were actually doing.
