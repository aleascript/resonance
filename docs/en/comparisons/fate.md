# Fate / Fate Core

**Fate created by:** Rob Donoghue & Fred Hicks — first published **2003**.  
**Fate Core:** **2013** — developed, authored, and edited by Leonard Balsera, Brian Engard, Jeremy Keller, Ryan Macklin, Mike Olson, Clark Valentine, Amanda Valentine, Fred Hicks, and Rob Donoghue.

## Nearby question

> **How can a fictionally meaningful sentence genuinely matter to the system?**

Fate uses **Aspects**: statements that are true in fiction, can create or remove permissions, can be invoked to affect a roll, or compelled to create complications and feed the Fate Point economy.

## What it makes possible

One sentence connects description, fictional permission, and mechanical economy. Players are encouraged to let defining traits complicate their characters' lives.

That power comes through a highly coherent and largely shared grammar: invocation, resource, bonus or reroll, compel.

## For Resonance

Fate strongly demonstrates that a sentence can **remain true and become mechanical**. Resonance can therefore ask a narrower question: when does the nature of fiction call not only for different content, but for **a different procedure that makes that content operative**?
