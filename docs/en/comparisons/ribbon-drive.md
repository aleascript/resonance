# Ribbon Drive — Avery Alder

## Nearby question

> **Can a cultural medium outside the rules text guide fiction without becoming a scenario?**

## Solution

*Ribbon Drive* is a structured freeform game organized around **playlists brought by the players**. Participants play a road trip, share narration of obstacles and scenery, and let music guide the journey and shape the unfolding story.

## What it makes possible

The playlist provides pacing, tone, association, and prompts without prescribing a sequence of scenes. Some inspirational burden is **externalized into material selected before play**, while remaining open to collective interpretation.

Music reduces some blank pages while still asking players to convert affect, lyrics, or atmosphere into fictional proposals.

## For Resonance

Ribbon Drive shows that a generative device can be **cultural and perceptual**, not only mechanical. It is a useful case for Situation and Vector research: provide direction and handles without writing the trajectory.
