# Dread

## Nearby question

> **How can tension and accumulating risk become directly perceptible to the player?**

## Solution

*Dread* replaces numerical resolution with a block tower. When an action is risky, pulling a block allows success but makes the tower more fragile for what follows. Refusing the pull accepts failure; collapse produces a catastrophic consequence for the character.

## What it makes possible

Difficulty is visible, tactile, and collective. Every success physically changes the conditions of later actions. The player does not merely imagine their character's tension; they perform a gesture that produces tension at the table.

The tower therefore carries part of the memory and progression of risk without extra calculation.

## For Resonance

Dread is an exemplary case of a **function externalized into a material support**. The comparison is not about reproducing the tower, but asking which property of an experience is better carried by an object than by memory, calculation, or interpretation alone.
