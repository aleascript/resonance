# Polaris

**Author:** Ben Lehman.  
**First published:** **2005**.

## Nearby question

> **Must resolution be a calculation or roll, or can it reside in the form of conversation itself?**

In Ben Lehman's *Polaris: Chivalric Tragedy at Utmost North*, authority is distributed among several roles around a scene's protagonist, including Heart, Mistaken, and Moons. Conflicts are structured through **ritual phrases** that allow acceptance, conditions, escalation, refusal, or concession.

## What it makes possible

Negotiation syntax becomes a mechanic. Tragedy is not only a theme; it is sustained by how participants are allowed to answer one another.

Burden normally carried by one GM is redistributed, although that redistribution asks players to learn and respect a precise conversational form.

## For Resonance

Polaris shows that **conversation itself can be a device**. It is an important case for asking not merely what rules resolve uncertainty, but what form of human interaction makes the intended experience practicable.
