# Ten Candles

## Nearby question

> **How can a foretold tragedy remain meaningful without making player choices irrelevant?**

## Solution

*Ten Candles* is a tragic-horror game in which the characters' final deaths are known. Ten candles literally light the session, act as a countdown, and structure its progression; character elements can also be physically burned.

## What it makes possible

Light, time, atmosphere, and information about the session's state are combined in one device. The final outcome is not the primary uncertainty: choices concern **what the characters do with the time they have left**.

The device also removes some responsibility from the GM for producing a tragic arc: material time makes that arc perceptible to everyone.

## For Resonance

Ten Candles shows that system need not reside only in local resolution. **The arc of an entire session can be carried by its material and temporal apparatus.**
