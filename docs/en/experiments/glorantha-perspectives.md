# Glorantha Perspectives

[**Play / read Glorantha Perspectives**](https://aleascript.github.io/glorantha-perspectives)

## Question tested

> **How can lived cosmologies and multiple relations to reality become playable without reducing them to one neutral hidden physics?**

## Burden targeted

Avoid forcing players either to learn a vast corpus before acting or to translate Glorantha into familiar contemporary categories.

## Complexity to preserve or generate

Cultures, myths, runes, religious practices, and worldviews must remain able to create real differences in what is thinkable, relevant, and possible.

## Device

The game uses Regard. What a character knows, owns, practices, or believes feeds relevance. Perspectives can become mechanically operative in resolution; Regard's language of **Prisms** owes much to this experiment.

## Observation

A perspective can do more than color narration: it can alter how uncertainty is processed. This offers one answer to **access to otherness** through practice rather than lore exposition alone.

## Burden shift

The approach depends on the quality of the perspectives provided. A Prism that is too prescriptive can caricature a cosmology; one that is too abstract can return all interpretive work to the player it was meant to support.

## Implication

Glorantha Perspectives is a major experimental case, but its local solutions are not Resonance invariants. The question of access to otherness must survive the invention of different devices.

## Status

**Playable game built with Regard; major experiment on perspective, ontology, and otherness.**
