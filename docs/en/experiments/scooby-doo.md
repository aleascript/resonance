# Scooby-Doo

[**Play / read Scooby-Doo**](https://aleascript.github.io/scooby-doo)

## Question tested

> **How much interface, rules, lore, and preparation can be removed while keeping an immediately recognizable experience?**

## Burden targeted

Lower player entry cost and preparation required to produce a strongly characterized session quickly.

## Complexity to preserve or generate

Play should still generate investigation, comic fear, character asymmetry, absurd plans, complications, and genre rhythm without trapping players in reenactment of known scenes.

## Device

Scooby-Doo uses a compressed form of Regard. Characters are created quickly and their tropes can become operative through strongly characterized Prisms.

## Observation

A small rules volume can transmit a strong signature when procedures directly orient the gestures that produce genre.

## Burden shift

Compression may hide dependence on prior knowledge of Scooby-Doo. Some accessibility may come from participants already knowing how this kind of story is supposed to work.

## Implication

The game is a useful test for distinguishing **generative simplicity** from mere economy of text. It should be tested with players who have very different levels of genre familiarity.

## Status

**Light game built with Regard; experiment on compression, genre, and entry cost.**
