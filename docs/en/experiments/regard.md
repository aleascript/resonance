# Regard

[**Play / read Regard**](https://aleascript.github.io/regard)

## Question tested

> **Can selecting what matters in fiction become the central resolution gesture without pre-encoding everything that may ever matter?**

## Burden targeted

Reduce searching for the right skill, modifier, or rule, and reduce the need to translate every fictional detail into a prior mechanical taxonomy.

## Complexity to preserve or generate

Fiction should remain open: relationships, objects, obligations, wounds, beliefs, or new circumstances must be able to matter without having been listed as system options beforehand.

## Device

Regard distinguishes **Intention**, **Focus**, and **Bets**. A Bet is a fictional truth made relevant by the situation. When different perspectives deserve to process uncertainty differently, **Prisms** can carry those perspectives.

## Observation

“What matters here?” acts as a compact semantic interface. It localizes complexity when it becomes relevant instead of modeling all of it in advance.

## Burden shift

This openness still requires judgment. Determining relevance can transfer work into table negotiation or facilitation if criteria are not sufficiently shareable.

## Implication

Regard is both a playable result and an experimental ground. Its architecture is not a mechanical definition of Resonance; other experiments must be able to answer the same questions through entirely different forms.

## Status

**Playable framework, provisional result, and active experimental ground.**
