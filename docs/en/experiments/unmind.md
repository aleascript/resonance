# Unmind

[**Play / read Unmind**](https://aleascript.github.io/unmind)

## Question tested

> **Can psychic unraveling emerge from the play device itself without using Regard's architecture?**

## Burden targeted

Avoid a heavy psychological model to learn or a generic mechanic that players would then have to reinterpret as loss, silence, or contamination.

## Complexity to preserve or generate

Characters should become psychologically ambiguous, relational, and contradictory; private perceptions should matter without the game having to establish one objective truth behind them.

## Device

Hidden fragments organized by depth; speaking reveals and loses a fragment; treatment imposes silence; silence can allow integration of another person's words; lost fragments become Traces reusable by the institution and location.

## Observation

Relevance can work through **erosion** rather than accumulation. Stacks, pill-token, revelation, and silence do not decorate a mechanic elsewhere: **the device is the mechanic**. The objective status of some private perceptions and cosmologies can remain undecidable while still producing concrete effects.

## Burden shift

The game externalizes part of memory and psychic erosion into objects and procedures. It may still demand emotional availability and careful attention to other players: procedural accessibility is not affective ease.

## Implication

Unmind shows that a Resonance experiment can abandon Regard's morphology while preserving the central problem: find a practicable form that lets the intended complexity emerge.

## Status

**Standalone game developed directly within Resonance; experiment on materiality, subjectivity, and loss.**
