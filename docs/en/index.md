# Resonance

**Resonance is a game design R&D laboratory devoted to tabletop role-playing games and adjacent forms of play.**

It does not define one universal rules engine. It asks questions about how experience, attention, fiction, procedures, play materials, and roles around the table shape one another—then builds games and devices to put those questions under pressure.

A role-playing game can never remove every layer between players and fiction. Rules, conversation, choices, uncertainty, interpretation, material interfaces, and distributions of authority are part of the medium. Resonance asks which of those layers are necessary for a given experience, which can be transformed, and what becomes possible when they are deliberately recomposed.

## Current status: a laboratory, not yet a Game Factory

Resonance has already produced concrete results: [**Regard**](https://aleascript.github.io/regard), a reusable TTRPG framework with a playable standard configuration; [**Glorantha Perspectives**](https://aleascript.github.io/glorantha-perspectives) and [**Scooby-Doo**](https://aleascript.github.io/scooby-doo), two games built with Regard; and [**Unmind**](https://aleascript.github.io/unmind), a standalone game developed directly through Resonance without using Regard.

That diversity is encouraging, but it is not yet enough to establish Resonance as a **Game Factory** in the strong sense.

> **Today, Resonance is the laboratory. The Game Factory is a hypothesis the laboratory is trying to test.**

The hypothesis is demanding: can one design method repeatedly help substantially different games emerge, possibly even from the same world, without all of them reproducing a hidden common architecture?

Regard and its games form a first experimental branch. Unmind provides stronger evidence that Resonance can survive the removal of that morphology. The corpus still needs to grow, counterexamples need to be sought, and the method needs to be confronted with other designers before the promise can be treated as established.

## A research program through play

Current work is organized into explicit [**research fields**](./research-fields): the boundaries of the role-playing medium, generating systems from experience, attention and perspective, material and alternative modes of play, facilitation and emergence, and entry accessibility.

The [**working principles**](./principles) describe what Resonance currently tries to preserve. The [**design axes**](./design-axes) are lenses for analyzing what a device asks players to do and what it asserts about fiction. The [**experiments**](./experiments) present the current lineage of games and frameworks and what each one tests.

Finally, [**comparisons**](./comparisons) place these questions in the broader history of role-playing—not to claim that Resonance invented questions already explored elsewhere, but to understand other games' solutions and sharpen what remains genuinely open.
